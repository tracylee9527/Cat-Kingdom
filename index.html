Pick an item, then tap a floor to place it. Cats ride the elevator or take the slower stairs to use everything you build. Coins come from cat tips, quests and goals. Cardboard comes from delivery boxes that arrive in the lobby.

Every quest you complete adds a new floor to the tower, and 2 new cats walk in from the street to live there.

Storehouse

Assign up to 3 cats to each themed floor. Workers make that floor's special product, and happier cats work faster. Tired or hungry workers take a break, then head back to their job.
